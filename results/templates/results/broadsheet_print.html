{% comment %} One class's master broadsheet, for paper. Rendered by `results.broadsheet_files.html_for()`. Everything comes off `sheet`, a `results.broadsheet.Sheet`, as text already worked out: this template adds no figure and drops none, and a missing paper is the "-" the sheet printed it as. `{{ }}` escapes names; nothing is `|safe`. Landscape, on the paper the person chose. `size` is one of two literals checked by `broadsheet_files`, and `font_pt` is a number it worked out so that every column fits the page's width: the table is as wide as the page and no wider. Everything in the table is in `em`, so one number scales the lot. The colour is the school's one (a six-digit hex by a database constraint), on the heading's rule and on the table's header rule only, as the cards do. A preview is not marked here: the stamp that says PREVIEW, NOT RELEASED is drawn onto every page afterwards (`class_print.stamp_pages`), in the page's own content, so a viewer cannot hide it.
