    Who we are

    Classnode is software that schools use to run results, fees, the register and messages to parents.

    Classnode is run by Classnode, RC number TODO: RC number. Our address is TODO: registered address.

    This notice is for parents, students, school staff and anyone who books a demo with us. It uses the words "controller" and "processor" as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) does. TODO: lawyer.

    The school decides. We process.

    Your child's school is the controller. The school decides what is recorded about its students, parents and staff, and what it is used for.

    Classnode is the processor. We store and handle that data for the school, on the school's instructions, and for nothing else.

    So if you are a parent, a student or a member of staff, your first contact for anything about your data is the school. We will help the school answer you. TODO: lawyer.

    Each school signs a data processing agreement with us. TODO: lawyer to draft the agreement and confirm it exists before launch.

    Classnode is the controller only for the details you give us when you book a demo, and for the records we keep to run and secure the service. TODO: lawyer.

    What we collect

    The school enters most of this. We do not ask children for it directly.

    About students

    • Name, admission number, class and the terms they were in each class.
    • Marks, grades, positions, teachers' remarks and released report cards.
    • Attendance: present, absent or late, by day.
    • At schools that use the Ogun State report card: height and weight at the start and end of each term, days missed through illness, and the illness. See children's data.
    • Fees: what was billed, paid, discounted or reversed, and the child's own bank account number for fees.

    About parents and guardians

    • Name, phone number and email address, and which children they are linked to.
    • The sign-in codes we send them, and when they were used.
    • Payments made into their child's fee account: the amount, the date and the bank's reference.

    About school staff

    • Name, email address or phone number, role at the school, and a password stored only as a secure hash.
    • What they did in Classnode where it matters later: who entered a mark, who released a result, who recorded or reversed a payment.

    About everyone who uses the service

    • The network address a request came from, and failed sign-in attempts, to stop people guessing passwords and codes.
    • A cookie that keeps you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
    • Error reports when something breaks. We remove personal data from these before they are sent.

    When you book a demo

    • Your name, school name, phone number, email address if you give it, number of students, and the network address the request came from.

    Why we use it

    • To produce results and report cards, and show them to the right parents.
    • To record fees and payments, put each payment on the right child's record, and send receipts.
    • To keep the register, and email a parent when their child is absent.
    • To send parents and staff sign-in codes, invitations and messages the school chooses to send.
    • To send the school's proprietor a daily summary of what came in and what is still owed.
    • To keep the service secure and working, and to fix errors.
    • To call you back when you book a demo.

    We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to train software. TODO: lawyer.

    Most of the people in Classnode's records are children. We take that seriously.

    We do not collect data from children directly. The school enters it.

    The school is responsible for having a parent's or guardian's consent where the law requires it. TODO: lawyer to confirm what section 31 of the NDPA requires and how the school records consent.

    A parent sees only their own children. A student who has their own sign-in sees only their own report cards. TODO: lawyer.

    Health records

    A school using the Ogun State report card may record a child's height and weight, days missed through illness, and what the illness was.

    This is health data about a child, so we hold it more tightly than anything else.

    Four people can see it: the child's class teacher for that term, the principal, the school's administrators, and the child's own parents or guardians once the term's report card is released.

    No other teacher, no other parent and no other school can see it. It is left out of class lists, broadsheets, exports and result checker slips.

    Once a term's report card is released, that term's health record cannot be changed. TODO: lawyer to confirm this is sensitive personal data under the NDPA and what more the school must do.
