    What Classnode does

    • Teachers enter marks. The principal checks and releases results. Parents see report cards.
    • Each child has a bank account number for fees. Payments into it go to the school's bank and onto the child's record.
    • Class teachers take the register. Parents of absent children get an email.
    • The proprietor gets a daily summary of what came in and what is still owed.

    We change and improve the service over time. We will not take away something a school depends on without telling the school first. TODO: lawyer and owner to set the notice period.

    Signing in

    • Staff sign in with a password. Parents sign in with a code sent to their phone or email.
    • Keep your password and codes to yourself. Do not let anyone else sign in as you.
    • If you think someone else has signed in as you, tell your school at once.
