About Classnode and how we protect your data

Who we are

Classnode is run by Classnode Edtech Limited, RC 9926572, based in {{ contact.location }}. We make school management software for secondary schools across Nigeria: results and report cards, fees, registers and CBT.

How we keep your data safe

  • Nightly off-site backups. {{ backups.line }}
  • Each school's data is kept separate. Your records are kept apart from every other school's.
  • Staff see only their part, by role. A teacher sees their own classes. The bursar sees fees. The principal sees the whole school.
  • Parents see only their own children. A parent can open a result only after the school has approved and released it.
