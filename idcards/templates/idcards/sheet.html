{% comment %} One run of up to ten children's ID cards as A4 pages: a page of fronts, then the page of backs. Rendered by `idcards.render.html_for()`. Every position and size is a finished number from `idcards.render`; nothing here does arithmetic. A card is 85.6 x 54 mm and holds nothing nearer than 3 mm to its edge. The cards touch, and the hairlines in the margin are the cut marks. **What a card says is what `Face` carries**, and a face has no parent's number and no field this template could print by mistake: the emergency line is the school's phone (`school.phone`), never a guardian's contact. `school.colour` is six hex digits by a database constraint (`results.look`), and `school.tint` is computed from it, which is what makes both safe to write into the stylesheet below. The colour is on the school's name, a thin rule and the initials; everything else is dark ink, so a school printing a class on a cheap printer does not streak. The font is the design's own Hanken Grotesk, with Noto Sans behind it for the Hausa letters and DejaVu Sans for anything else, as the report card has. {% endcomment %} ID cards, {{ class_name }} {% for page in pages %}
