What this does

Creates the classes your school runs this session, such as JSS 1A or SS 2 Science.

Why it matters

Every register, mark sheet and report card belongs to a class. You can only import students once their class exists.

What to do

  1. Pick the levels you run, JSS 1 to SS 3.
  2. Add the arms for each level, such as A, B and C.
  3. Save. You can rename or add a class later.
