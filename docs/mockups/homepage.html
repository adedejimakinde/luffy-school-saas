Questions

What schools ask us.

Do our teachers need laptops?

No. Classnode works in the browser on the phones your teachers already carry. There is nothing to install.

What happens when the network is bad?

Scores and registers stay on the phone and send when the connection comes back. Teachers can keep working in the meantime.

Can we use the Ogun State report sheet?

Yes. Switch on the Ogun State template in setup and your report cards follow the sheet the Ministry and OGSERA ask for.

Can we bring our current student list?

Yes. Send it as an Excel or CSV file and it is imported into your classes. We help you with it during setup.

How do parents pay school fees?

By bank transfer, the way they already do. Each child has their own account number, so the payment shows on that child's record.

Who can see our school's records?

Only your staff, each according to their role, and parents for their own children. Your records are kept apart from every other school's. Our privacy notice has the details.
