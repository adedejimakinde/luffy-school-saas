## What changes in second term

Second term is shorter, and most schools add one item to the fee list: the inter-house sports levy. Set it up **before** the first day, not after the first payment arrives.

## Three things to settle first

- The amount for each class, written down and signed off by the principal.
- Whether a sibling discount applies to the new item.
- The last day to pay without a reminder.

> A fee set late is a fee parents argue about.
