Five checks before you release first term report cards

A short list a principal can run on the Friday before cards go home, so no parent finds a missing mark or a wrong total first.

KO
Kemi Oladipo

14 Oct 20265 min read

Report cards go home once a term, and every parent reads them closely. A missing mark or a total that does not add up is the first thing they notice, and the school hears about it at the gate on Monday.

These five checks take about an hour on the Friday before the cards are printed.

1. Every subject has a mark for every student

Open each class's broadsheet and look for blanks. A blank is either a student who missed the exam or a teacher who has not finished entering marks. They need different answers, so find out which before you print.

2. The totals add up

Pick three students in each class: the top, the middle and the bottom. Add their marks by hand. If the sheet and your sum agree for all three, the rest will too.

Checking three cards by hand catches more than reading thirty on screen.

3. Attendance is filled in

The sheet asks for the times school opened and the times the student was present. Both come from the register, so a class with gaps in its register will have gaps here.

4. Remarks are written, and written for this child

  • One sentence from the class teacher about the term.
  • One sentence from the principal.
  • No remark copied across a whole class.

5. The names are spelt the way the parent spells them

Check the admission list against the forms parents filled in. A wrong name on a report card is remembered long after the marks are forgotten.
