Second CA test: algebra, indices and logs

Mathematics, SS 2A and SS 2B. 20 questions.

Class testYour questions, your timing. For CA tests and quizzes.UTME-style practiceEnglish and three subjects, one timer, the CBT centre screen.WASSCE-style objectivesOne subject per paper, its own timer, options A to D.

Timing

Fair for every student

  • Shuffle question orderEach student gets the questions in their own order.
  • Shuffle option orderA for one student may be C for the next. Marking follows the option, not the letter.
  • One attemptA second attempt only for a student you name, with a reason. It is logged.
  • Students can go back to a questionOff means each question is answered once, in order.
  • Question number gridShows which are answered and flagged.
  • Calculator on screen

Signing in to sit it

  • Test codeSix letters you read out or write on the board when the sitting starts. You can change it at any time.
  • Exam PIN from the student's slipProves the student is who the admission number says. Turning it off is allowed only for a test whose scores cannot be sent to CA; the school's server refuses the send otherwise.

This does not lock the browser. Supervise the room; the log shows when each student started, dropped off and submitted.

After the test

  • Scores are released by youNobody sees a score until you release it: students their own, parents their own child's.
    Always
  • Corrections with released scoresEach student sees their own answers, the right answer and your explanation.
