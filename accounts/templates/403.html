This project has shipped that bug before. ## Why this file exists at all Issue #122. `SchoolAccessMiddleware` raises `PermissionDenied` carrying a sentence written for the person it refuses, and with no `403.html` here Django's default handler rendered `ERROR_PAGE_TEMPLATE` with empty `details` and discarded it. A reader saw "403 Forbidden" and learned neither what had happened nor whether anything would fix it. ## No stylesheet, no modules, no `{% load static %}` Deliberately. This is the page that has to render when something is already wrong, and every asset it depended on would be another thing that can be missing at exactly the moment it is needed. The styling below is inline and small enough to read. ## The link, and why it carries a hostname `/staff-sign-in/` is mounted in `urls_public.py` and **not** in `urls.py`, so it exists on the portal and nowhere else — while this page is being rendered on a school's host, which is where the refusal happened. A relative link would resolve against the school's host and hand the reader a 404 from the one screen whose job is telling them how to get back in. That is not hypothetical: it is the dead `` the card page shipped, which `schools.hosts .portal_host()` was lifted out of `results.views` to fix. Scheme-relative — `//host/path` — rather than a hard-coded `https://`, which is the form `static/card/states.js` `wayBack()` already uses: it keeps whatever scheme this deployment is being served over instead of pinning one that is wrong in development. `portal_host` is empty when no primary `Domain` exists for the public schema, and then there is no link at all, only the sentence. A dead link is worse than being told to go back the way you came. {% endcomment %}
